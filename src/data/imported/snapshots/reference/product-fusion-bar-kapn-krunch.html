Fusion Bar Kap’N Krunch

Fusion Bar Kap’N Krunch is a cereal-inspired infused chocolate bar that combines sweet milk chocolate with crunchy cereal clusters, designed to mimic the nostalgic taste of breakfast cereals while delivering a functional or infused edible experience.

👉 It’s part of the Fusion lineup of dessert-style mushroom chocolate bars known for bold flavors and textured bites.

🥣 Flavor Profile Fusion Bar Kap’N Krunch

This bar is built around a sweet, crunchy cereal experience:

  • Creamy milk chocolate base
  • Crunchy cereal-style clusters
  • Sweet vanilla and caramel notes
  • Light toasted grain flavor

👉 The overall taste is often described as “cereal milk chocolate with extra crunch”

🌿 Ingredients & Blend Fusion Bar Kap’N Krunch

Depending on the version, Kap’N Krunch bars typically include:

  • Chocolate base (milk or white chocolate)
  • Crunch cereal inclusions
  • Mushroom blend such as:
    • Lion’s Mane (focus)
    • Reishi (calm)
    • Cordyceps (energy)

👉 Some versions are marketed as functional wellness bars, while others may differ depending on formulation.
