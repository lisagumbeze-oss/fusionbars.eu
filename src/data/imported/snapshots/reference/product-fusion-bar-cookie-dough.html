Fusion Bar Cookie Dough

Fusion Bar Cookie Dough Cookie Dough (from Fusion Mushroom Bars) is a chocolate edible infused with mushroom blends, designed to recreate the classic raw Cookie Dough flavor in a smooth, easy-to-consume.

👉 It combines dessert-style taste + infused ingredients in one product.

🍫 Flavor Profile Fusion Bar Cookie Dough

This is one of the most popular flavors because of its rich, nostalgic taste:

  • Sweet brown sugar & vanilla notes
  • Soft cookie dough-like texture
  • Chocolate chips or cocoa hints
  • Slight earthy undertone (from infusion)

👉 Many describe it as tasting like raw cookie dough covered in chocolate

🌿 Ingredients & Blend Fusion Bar Cookie Dough

Typical versions include:

  • Chocolate base (milk or dark)
  • Cookie dough flavoring
  • Functional mushrooms like:
    • Lion’s Mane (focus)
    • Reishi (calm)
    • Cordyceps (energy)
    • Chaga (immune support)

👉 Some versions are marketed as wellness-focused, while others may be psychoactive depending on formulation.

⚡ Effects (Important) Fusion Bar Cookie Dough

Effects depend on what type of bar you have:

🟢 Functional versions:

  • Mild focus and clarity
  • Relaxation
  • Balanced energy

🔴 Psychoactive versions:

  • Euphoria
  • Mood shifts
  • Altered perception

👉 Effects usually begin within 30–90 minutes

📊 Dosing Fusion Bar Cookie Dough

Most bars are divided into small squares:

  • 1 piece: light / beginner
  • 2–3 pieces: moderate
  • 4+ pieces: stronger effects

👉 Always start low and wait before increasing

✨ Key Features

  • Dessert-style flavor (very beginner-friendly)
  • Easy portion control
  • Portable and discreet
  • Often marketed with even dosing per square
